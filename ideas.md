## Idea A — Editorial Magazine

**Design Movement:** High-end architectural editorial (Dezeen / Wallpaper* aesthetic)

**Core Principles:**
- Asymmetric editorial grid — content bleeds to edges, images are oversized and dominant
- Typography does the heavy lifting — large display type contrasts with small refined body copy
- Restraint as luxury — generous white space, nothing extraneous
- Photography-first — renders are treated as hero editorial images, not thumbnails

**Color Philosophy:**
- Near-white background (#F7F5F2 — warm off-white, not clinical white)
- Deep charcoal text (#1C1C1A) for headings
- Warm sand accent (#C8B89A) for dividers and highlights — echoes the renovation palette
- No gradients, no shadows — flat, editorial

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed hero image with large overlaid title
- Alternating left/right image-text blocks with asymmetric proportions (60/40, 70/30)
- Sticky side navigation for space sections
- Horizontal scroll gallery for before/after comparisons

**Signature Elements:**
- Large section numbers in light grey behind headings (01, 02, 03...)
- Thin horizontal rules as section dividers
- Pull quotes in large italic type

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Scroll-triggered fade-ins for content blocks
- Image hover: subtle scale (1.02) with slow ease
- Navigation: smooth scroll with active section highlight

**Animation:**
- Entrance: content slides up 20px with opacity fade, 0.6s ease-out
- Images: parallax scroll effect on hero images
- No bouncy or playful animations — everything is slow and deliberate

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (serif, elegant, editorial)
- Body: DM Sans (clean, modern, readable)
- Hierarchy: 72px display / 14px caps label / 18px body

## Idea B — Dark Luxury Portfolio

**Design Movement:** Dark mode luxury portfolio (Zaha Hadid Architects / high-end property developer aesthetic)

**Core Principles:**
- Dark background creates drama and makes renders pop with maximum contrast
- Gold/brass accent colour echoes premium materials
- Minimal navigation — everything is one long scroll
- Renders are the hero — the design steps back to let them shine

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep near-black background (#0F0E0C — warm dark, not cold)
- Warm gold accent (#B8965A) for headings, dividers, highlights
- Light cream text (#E8E0D4) for body copy
- Renders appear to float on the dark background

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-viewport hero with animated title reveal
- Masonry-style image grid for the space gallery
- Wide single-column layout with generous margins
- Floating room navigation pills

**Signature Elements:**
- Gold horizontal rule dividers
- Room labels in small gold caps
- Subtle noise/grain texture on dark background sections

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Cursor changes to crosshair on image hover
- Images expand to full-screen lightbox on click
- Smooth section transitions

**Animation:**
- Title: character-by-character reveal on load
- Section entrance: fade in from below
- Image hover: warm golden overlay at 10% opacity

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (classic serif, luxury)
- Body: Outfit (geometric sans, modern)
- Hierarchy: 80px display / 11px gold caps label / 16px body

## Idea C — Warm Minimal Studio

**Design Movement:** Warm Scandinavian studio (Norm Architects / Kinfolk aesthetic)

**Core Principles:**
- Warmth through material references — the design palette echoes the renovation (oak, stone, linen)
- Horizontal rhythm — wide layouts, landscape images, breathing room
- Craft and detail — small typographic refinements, considered spacing
- The presentation feels like a printed design document brought to screen

**Color Philosophy:**
- Warm linen background (#F2EDE6) — references the carpet and wall tones
- Deep warm brown (#2C2420) for headings — references the oak timber
- Muted sage green (#7A8C7E) as accent — echoes the NICKEBO kitchen cabinets
- Warm grey (#8C8680) for secondary text

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Wide horizontal sections with images spanning full width
- Two-column layout: image left, text right (or reversed) with generous padding
- Sticky top navigation with room names
- Colour palette section as a horizontal swatch row

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin sage green vertical rule beside section headings
- Material swatches rendered as small circles with labels
- Room status badges (Locked In / In Progress / Pending)

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states use warm transitions (colour shifts toward sage green)
- Images open in a clean lightbox
- Navigation highlights current section as user scrolls

**Animation:**
- Entrance: content fades in with 0.4s ease, no movement
- Image hover: very subtle brightness increase (1.05)
- Navigation: smooth scroll, active item gets sage green left border

**Typography System:**
- Display: Fraunces (variable serif, warm, editorial)
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans (clean, contemporary)
- Hierarchy: 56px display / 12px sage caps label / 17px body
